Kakuromino – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Kakuromino (package name com.madhoux.kakuromino, "the game") uses, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer, Madhoux ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or any account. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads, offers optional in-app purchases and an optional online duel mode; the third-party services behind these features process some data as described below.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (solved levels, times, crowns, coins, items you own, settings, streaks, quests and statistics) is saved in the game's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Other ad networks, such as AppLovin, may bid for ad space through AdMob's mediation. To show and measure ads, these services may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks, completed videos).

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch players in these regions are asked through Google's consent message whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options. Some US states: where the law requires it, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information in the same way.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads on most Android phones).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy, AppLovin Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (for example "Remove ads", coin packs or cosmetic sets) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives which product was bought, so it can unlock it and restore it after a reinstall. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Online duel (Google Firebase)

The duel mode is optional. When you create or join a duel, the game signs in to Google Firebase with an anonymous account (a random ID, no name, e-mail or phone number) and stores the duel room in a Firebase Realtime Database hosted in the EU (europe-west1). A room contains: the room code, the random player IDs, the short player name shown to your opponent, the chosen time and difficulty, ready state, the number of solved puzzles and timestamps. Rooms are deleted when the match ends, and leftover rooms are deleted after about one hour. Firebase also processes your IP address to provide the service. See the Firebase privacy information.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example "today's puzzle is ready") directly on your phone. No server and no personal data are involved. You can turn notifications off in the game or in your phone's settings.

6. Internet connection check

Before a rewarded video, the game sends a short request to Google's connectivity check address (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com) to see whether you are online. No game data is sent with it.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and do not collect it for ourselves.
  • The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

8. Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalized ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalized ads, purchases and the duel mode are based on providing the game you chose to use and on our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to object to it, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, requests about ad or Firebase data are best sent to the providers above; we will gladly help.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the online duel, contact us and we will delete the related data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
